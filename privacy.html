Rowline

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Rowline is a knitting and crochet pattern reader and row counter made by Raşit Şahin. It is built so that your projects stay yours.

What stays on your device

Your projects, patterns (PDFs, photos and scans), counters, notes, drawings and project photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Rowline, they are also synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. The developer cannot see this data.

Pattern text is read with Apple’s on-device text recognition and on-device language model. Nothing is uploaded for this.

No account, no tracking, no ads

Rowline has no sign-up, contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To check whether Rowline Pro is unlocked, the app may use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a paper pattern. Photos you pick are stored in the project you add them to.

Children

Rowline does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a project in the app, or delete the app. iCloud copies can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Rowline.

Contact

Questions: mrrasitsahin@icloud.com
